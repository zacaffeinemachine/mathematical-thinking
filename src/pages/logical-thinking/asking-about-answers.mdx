We are back on the Isles of Paradox. Every inhabitant there is an **oracle**
or a **chimera** and nothing else: an oracle's every sentence is true, and a
chimera's every sentence is false.

A ruby has been hidden in one of two boxes, the **Coral** box or the
**Amber** box. Phoebe, Selene and Helios are three inhabitants who all know
which box holds it, each of them knows what kind the other two are, and we
are told that **exactly two of the three are oracles**. They say:

- **Phoebe says:** *"If Selene were asked, 'Is the ruby in the Coral box?',
  she would answer yes."*
- **Selene says:** *"If Helios were asked, 'Is Phoebe a chimera?', he would
  answer yes."*
- **Helios says:** *"The ruby is in the Coral box precisely when Selene is an
  oracle."*

**Who is the chimera, and where is the ruby?**

Notice that Phoebe and Selene have not said where the ruby is, and they have
not said what anybody is. Each has reported what somebody else *would*
answer, and keeping those two things apart is the work of this section.
