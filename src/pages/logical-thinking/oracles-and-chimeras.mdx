**All six puzzles below are set on the Isles of Paradox.** Every inhabitant
there is an **oracle** or a **chimera** and nothing else: an oracle's every
sentence is true, and a chimera's every sentence is false. This holds in all
six, and is not repeated in each one.

## 1. Five on the Shore

Five figures stand on the shore: Elara, Orion, Phoebe, Selene, and Helios.
Each makes a single statement.

- **Elara:** *"Orion is a chimera."*
- **Orion:** *"Exactly two of us are oracles."*
- **Phoebe:** *"Orion and Elara are either both oracles or both chimeras."*
- **Selene:** *"Phoebe and Elara are either both oracles or both chimeras."*
- **Helios:** *"Selene and Phoebe are either both oracles or both chimeras."*

**Identify who is an oracle and who is a chimera.**

## 2. Oracles and Chimeras

Three figures stand before us: Ariadne, Selene, and Thalia. Each makes a
single statement.

- **Ariadne says:** *"Selene is an oracle."*
- **Selene says:** *"All three of us are oracles."*
- **Thalia says:** *"Ariadne is a chimera."*

**Who is an oracle, and who is a chimera?**

## 3. Treacherous Trio

Again three figures stand before us: Ariadne, Selene, and Thalia. Each makes
a single statement.

- **Ariadne says:** *"There is exactly one chimera amongst the three of us,
  and it is not me."*
- **Selene says:** *"Ariadne is an oracle."*
- **Thalia says:** *"Ariadne and Selene are both oracles."*

**Who is an oracle, and who is a chimera?**

## 4. Counting Chimeras

Ten beings are lined up in a row, each one either a chimera or an oracle.

- The first being says, *"Exactly **one** of us ten is a chimera."*
- The second being says, *"Exactly **two** of us ten are chimeras."*
- The third being says, *"Exactly **three** of us ten are chimeras."*
- and so on, down to
- The tenth being says, *"Exactly **ten** of us ten are chimeras."*

**How many chimeras are there?**

## 5. Counting Chimeras Again

word has changed.

- The first being says, *"**At least one** of us ten is a chimera."*
- The second being says, *"**At least two** of us ten are chimeras."*
- The third being says, *"**At least three** of us ten are chimeras."*
- and so on, down to
- The tenth being says, *"**At least ten** of us ten are chimeras."*

## 6. An Endless Queue

Endlessly many beings are lined up in a queue, each one either a chimera or
an oracle.

- The first being says, *"The being behind me is an oracle."*
- The second being says, *"The being behind me is a chimera."*
- The third being says, *"The being behind me is an oracle."*
- The fourth being says, *"The being behind me is a chimera."*
- and so on for ever, the two sentences alternating.

It is known that **the first being is an oracle**. What is each of the
following?
