**All five puzzles below are set on the Isles of Paradox.** Every inhabitant
there is an **oracle** or a **chimera** and nothing else: an oracle's every
sentence is true, and a chimera's every sentence is false. This holds in all
five, and is not repeated in each one.

## 1. One Answer, Two Ways

A ruby lies in the **Coral** box or the **Amber** box, and Thalia knows which.

- **a)** You ask Thalia, *"Is the ruby in the Coral box?"*, and she answers
  yes.
- **b)** You ask Thalia, *"Is your answer to 'Is the ruby in the Coral box?'
  yes?"*, and she answers yes.

The two are separate situations, not two things that happen one after
another. In each, **where is the ruby?**

## 2. Two Speakers, Three Boxes

Ariadne and Thalia stand before three boxes, **Coral**, **Amber** and
**Topaz**, one of which holds the ruby. Both of them know which, and each
knows what kind the other is. Nothing at all is known about their kinds.

- **Ariadne says:** *"Thalia's answer to 'Is the ruby in the Coral box?' is
  yes."*
- **Thalia says:** *"Ariadne's answer to 'Is the ruby in the Amber box?' is
  yes."*

**Where is the ruby, and what can be said about the two speakers?**

## 3. Two Oracles and a Chimera

Ariadne, Selene and Thalia stand before the **Coral** box and the **Amber**
box, one of which holds the ruby. All three know where the ruby is, each
knows what kind the other two are, and **exactly two of the three are
oracles**.

- **Ariadne says:** *"Selene's answer to 'Is the ruby in the Coral box?' is
  yes."*
- **Selene says:** *"Thalia's answer to 'Is the ruby in the Coral box?' is
  yes."*
- **Thalia says:** *"Ariadne is an oracle."*

**Who is the chimera, and where is the ruby?**

## 4. How Many Boxes

The ruby is hidden in one of a row of boxes, and a single islander of unknown
kind stands before them and knows which box holds it. You may ask that
islander four questions, one after another, each answerable only by yes or
no, and you may choose each question after hearing the answer to the one
before.

**What is the largest number of boxes you could deal with this way**, always
finding the ruby whatever the answers turn out to be?

<PuzzleAnswer puzzle={howManyBoxes} client:visible />

## 5. Three at the Boxes

Phoebe, Selene and Helios stand before the **Coral** box and the **Amber**
box, one of which holds the ruby. All three know where the ruby is, each
knows what kind the other two are, and **exactly two of the three are
oracles**.

- **Phoebe says:** *"Selene's answer to 'Is the ruby in the Coral box?' is
  yes."*
- **Selene says:** *"Helios's answer to 'Is Phoebe a chimera?' is yes."*
- **Helios says:** *"The ruby is in the Coral box precisely when Selene is an
  oracle."*

**Who is the chimera, and where is the ruby?**
