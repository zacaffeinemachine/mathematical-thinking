<Problem n={3} title="Two words that share nothing" alphabet="the 26 lowercase letters">
Design a machine that accepts `hate` and `qrmt`, and nothing else.

<Problem n={4} title="Three words, two of which start alike" alphabet="the 26 lowercase letters">
Design a machine that accepts `start`, `stop` and `pause`, and nothing else.

`start` and `stop` share their first two letters, so their corridors should
share circles until they part. Do not draw two separate corridors and hope
nobody notices.

Draw one arrow labelled `x` for "any other letter" rather than drawing
twenty-six of them.
<Tests accept={["start", "stop", "pause"]} reject={["", "st", "sto", "starts", "stopp", "paus", "startstop"]} />
</Problem>

<Problem n={5} title="Words that are prefixes of each other" alphabet="the 26 lowercase letters">

<Problem n={6} title="Every short word" alphabet="the 26 lowercase letters">

<Problem n={7} title="A word at the start, anything after" hard alphabet="the 26 lowercase letters">
