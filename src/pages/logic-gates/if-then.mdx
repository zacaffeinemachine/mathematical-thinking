Every box on this page checks an if-then claim. Its bulb is lit when the claim
holds, and dark when the claim is broken, which happens only when the if-part
holds and the then-part fails.

<p style={{display:'flex', flexWrap:'wrap', alignItems:'center', gap:'6px 14px'}}>
  <span><Switch on label="up" /> is $\texttt{1}$</span>
  <span><Switch label="down" /> is $\texttt{0}$</span>
  <span><Bulb lit /> claim holds</span>
  <span><Bulb /> claim broken</span>
</p>
