The animations of Hilbert's hotel now sit on the lesson page itself,
each one next to the paragraph it illustrates. Each has its own
Play, Next and Back buttons, and a box underneath where you can ask
your own question.

1. [A hotel with no last room](../hilbert-hotel/#explainer-no-last-room).
   Walk down the corridor to any room you name, even room
   $1{,}000{,}000$, and see the room after it.
2. [One more guest](../hilbert-hotel/#explainer-one-more-guest).
   Follow any guest one door along, or ask who ends up in a given
   room.
3. [A bus full of guests](../hilbert-hotel/#explainer-busload).
   Watch the idea that fails, then the plan that works. Follow any
   guest to room $2n$ or any passenger to room $2k - 1$.
4. [A fleet of buses](../hilbert-hotel/#explainer-fleet).
   The diagonal thread empties the fleet into one coach. Pick any
   bus and seat and see which coach seat and which room that
   passenger gets.
5. [Reducing to a problem already solved](../hilbert-hotel/#explainer-reduction).
   The kettle joke, and the register of the whole night printed
   from one formula.
