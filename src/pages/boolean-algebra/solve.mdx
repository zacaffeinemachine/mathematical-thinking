Find every assignment of $\texttt{0}$ and $\texttt{1}$ to the letters that
makes all of the equations hold at once. The problems get harder as you go
down the page.

Under each problem, type the set of all solutions and press **Submit**.
Write each solution as the values of the letters in order, so that in the
letters $a$, $b$ the solution $a = \texttt{0}$, $b = \texttt{1}$ is
`(0, 1)`, and a set of two solutions is `{(0, 1), (1, 0)}`. A system may
have no solutions at all, and then the answer is the empty set, `{}`. You
are told only whether your set is exactly right.

Checking a pattern by substituting it is always allowed, and it is a good
habit once you have an answer. The aim, though, is to *find* the solutions
with the moves of the chapter, and to know that none are missing.
