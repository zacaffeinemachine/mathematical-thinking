Every puzzle on this page can be solved with the method of this chapter.
Give each unknown a yes/no letter, turn each clue into an equation, and solve
the system. The puzzles get harder as you go down the page.

Fill in the sheet under each puzzle and press **Submit**. You are told only
whether the whole sheet is right.

Several puzzles are set on the **Isles of Paradox**. Every inhabitant there is
an **oracle** or a **chimera** and nothing else: an oracle's every sentence is
true, and a chimera's every sentence is false.

## 1. At the Jetty

Two islanders, Ariadne and Selene, stand at the jetty. Only Ariadne speaks.

- **Ariadne:** *"At least one of us two is a chimera."*

**What is each of them?**

## 2. Three Switches

A hall has three lamps, A, B and C, and three switches. Flipping a switch
changes each lamp it is wired to: a lamp that was on goes off, and a lamp that
was off comes on.

- Switch 1 is wired to lamps A and B.
- Switch 2 is wired to lamps B and C.
- Switch 3 is wired to lamps A, B and C.

All three lamps start off. The caretaker flips some of the switches, each one
at most once, and afterwards **only lamp B is on**.

**Which switches did the caretaker flip?**

<PuzzleAnswer puzzle={threeSwitches} client:visible />

## 3. Three at the Well

Three islanders, Ariadne, Selene and Thalia, stand at the well. Each makes a
single statement.

- **Ariadne:** *"Selene and Thalia are the same kind."*
- **Selene:** *"Ariadne and I are different kinds."*
- **Thalia:** *"At least two of us three are oracles."*

**What is each of them?**

<PuzzleAnswer puzzle={threeAtTheWell} client:visible />

## 4. Three Caskets

A portrait is locked inside exactly one of three caskets, one of gold, one of
silver and one of lead. Each casket bears an inscription.

- **Gold:** *"The portrait is in this casket."*
- **Silver:** *"The portrait is not in this casket."*
- **Lead:** *"The portrait is not in the gold casket."*

You are told that **at most one of the three inscriptions is true**.

**Where is the portrait, and which inscription is true?**

<PuzzleAnswer puzzle={threeCaskets} client:visible />

## 5. The Last Biscuit

The last biscuit has gone from the tin, and exactly one of four siblings took
it. Each says one thing.

- **Fern:** *"Gus took it."*
- **Gus:** *"Ivy took it."*
- **Hugo:** *"I did not take it."*
- **Ivy:** *"Gus is lying."*

**Exactly one of the four is telling the truth.** Who took the biscuit, and
who told the truth?

<PuzzleAnswer puzzle={theLastBiscuit} client:visible />

## 6. Exam Results

Jonas, Kira and Lena have each either passed or failed an exam. By a strange
coincidence, each one who passed tells the truth and each one who failed
lies.

- **Jonas:** *"Kira failed."*
- **Kira:** *"Lena and I both passed."*
- **Lena:** *"Jonas and I did not both pass."*

**Who passed and who failed?**

<PuzzleAnswer puzzle={examResults} client:visible />

## 7. The Concert

Five friends, Alma, Bruno, Clara, Dario and Elena, are deciding who goes to a
concert. Here is what we know.

- At least one of Alma and Bruno goes.
- Exactly one of Alma and Clara goes.
- Clara and Elena either both go or both stay home.
- At least one of Dario and Elena goes.
- Exactly two of the five go.

**Who goes to the concert?**

<PuzzleAnswer puzzle={theConcert} client:visible />

## 8. Four Doors, Three Guards

A prize is behind exactly one of four doors, numbered 1 to 4. Three guards
each make two claims about it.

- **First guard:** *"It is behind door 1 or door 2."* and *"It is behind door 3."*
- **Second guard:** *"It is not behind door 1."* and *"It is behind door 4."*
- **Third guard:** *"It is behind door 2."* and *"It is not behind door 4."*

**Each guard has made exactly one true claim and one false one.**

**Which door hides the prize?**

<PuzzleAnswer puzzle={fourDoors} client:visible />

## 9. The Fourth Voice

Four islanders, Ariadne, Selene, Thalia and Dione, each make a single
statement.

- **Ariadne:** *"At least one of us four is a chimera."*
- **Selene:** *"Ariadne and Thalia are not both oracles."*
- **Thalia:** *"Ariadne and Selene are different kinds."*
- **Dione:** *"Selene and I are different kinds."*

**What is each of them?** Mark as undetermined whatever the statements do not
settle.

<PuzzleAnswer puzzle={theFourthVoice} client:visible />

## 10. Six Lamps in a Row

Six lamps stand in a row, numbered 1 to 6 from the left, and under each lamp
is a button. Pressing a button flips its own lamp and the lamps immediately
beside it: one neighbour for a lamp at either end, two for any other. Here the
lit lamps are drawn ● and the unlit ones ○.

<div class="lamps">● ○ ○ ● ○ ●</div>

**Put every lamp out, pressing each button at most once.** There is exactly
one way to do it. Which buttons do you press?

- **Leander:** *"Iris and Nyx are both chimeras."*

## 12. A Card of Sentences

A card has four sentences written on it, and nothing else.

1. *"Exactly one sentence on this card is false."*
2. *"Exactly two sentences on this card are false."*
3. *"Exactly three sentences on this card are false."*
4. *"Exactly four sentences on this card are false."*

**Which sentences are true and which are false?**

You are also told that **at least one of the six is an oracle**. Ariadne is
one of the six.
