Simplify each expression as far as it will go. Every one of them looks worse
than it is, and every one ends up short. The problems get harder as you go
down the page.

The tools are the familiar laws (order, grouping, multiplying out) and the two
that belong to this world alone: a value added to itself gives
$\texttt{0}$, and a value times itself gives itself back.

**What counts as finished.** Multiply out every bracket, remove every power,
and let every repeated term cancel. What is left is a sum of products of
different letters, possibly with a $\texttt{1}$ among them, or else the
single value $\texttt{0}$. Every expression has exactly one finished form,
up to the order in which you write things, and only that form is accepted.

**How to type an answer.** Use the letters, the digits `0` and `1`, and `+`.
A product is written by putting the letters side by side, as in
`ab + c`, or with `*`, as in `a*b + c`.
